Refunds & withdrawal

Plain-language information about subscription cancellation, refunds and EU withdrawal rights for Jyson AI

Last updated: 7 November 2026
PrivacyTermsRefundsTrackingSecurityAccessibility
Paid plans are not on sale yet. During the release every new account gets all features free until 30 November 2027, so there is nothing to pay or refund. This page describes how it will work once paid plans open.

1. Stopping renewal

Paid Jyson plans are subscriptions processed by Stripe. You can stop renewal at any time on the Plan tab of your account (or with Cancel membership in the app, which opens it). Nothing more is charged, and the plan keeps working until the end of the period you already paid for. You can turn renewal back on before then.

2. Withdrawing within 14 days (EU)

If you are a consumer in the EU, you can withdraw from a new subscription within 14 days of buying it, without giving a reason. Use the Withdraw and get a refund button on the Plan tab; it shows the amount before you confirm. You can also tell us by email with a clear statement that you withdraw.

  • What you get back: at checkout you ask for your plan to start right away. Because of that, if you withdraw you pay only for the days you used, and the rest of the payment is refunded (EU Consumer Rights Directive, article 14(3)). If a plan was started without that request, the whole payment is refunded.
  • When: the refund goes back to the card you paid with, normally within 14 days of your withdrawal; your bank may take a few more days to show it.
  • Your plan: paid features end as soon as you withdraw, and your account goes back to Free.
  • Renewals: the 14 days apply to a new subscription, not to each automatic renewal. For a renewal, stop renewal instead.
  • Repeated withdrawals: if an account has withdrawn twice in the last 12 months, the button sends you to support and a person handles it. Your right to withdraw stays the same.

3. Refund requests

Duplicate charges, billing errors, unauthorized charges and service failures will be reviewed in line with applicable law and Stripe records. Statutory refund rights take priority over project policy.

4. Support

For a refund, withdrawal, cancellation or billing question, email Jysonsupport@gmail.com from the email used for your account or purchase. Include a brief description and an invoice reference if available. Never send passwords, API keys or full payment-card details. See Support for the available contact channels.
